# Demo video script template

Length: 60 to 180 seconds. Total beats below sum to roughly 150 seconds. Cut beats to compress.

## Beat 1, hook (0:00 to 0:10)

Open with one sentence that names the problem and the user. No logo splash. No "Hi we are X". The viewer is already losing attention; the first 10 seconds either earn the next 30 or do not.

Slot:

> <name the user>, <name the painful thing they currently do>. <Project name> changes that.

## Beat 2, solution snapshot (0:10 to 0:30)

Cut directly to a short demo clip, 5 to 10 seconds, of the load-bearing flow's success state. No narration over the clip if the visuals carry it.

Slot:

> <screen recording of the load-bearing demo flow's outcome, the "after" state>

## Beat 3, walkthrough (0:30 to 1:30)

Walk the load-bearing flow from start to finish. The clip must show the sponsor integration doing real work (the Walrus blob being retrieved, the DeepBook order being placed and settled, the Scallop deposit + borrow + repay).

Slot:

> Step one, <action>. Step two, <action>. Step three, <action with the sponsor integration visibly working>.

## Beat 4, team (1:30 to 2:00, optional)

One beat on the team if the team is itself a credibility signal (prior shipping experience, domain expertise, prior hackathon wins). Skip if the team is new; let the work speak.

## Beat 5, ask (2:00 to 2:30)

Tell the viewer where to go next. Demo URL, repo URL, contact. Keep this short.

Slot:

> Try it at <demo URL>. Code at <repo URL>. <Optional: a single ask: "looking for design feedback" or "open to grant intros">.

## Production notes

- Record at 1080p or higher, export at the deepsurge.xyz spec.
- Use a real microphone if voiceover is included. Built-in laptop mics are read as low effort.
- Cut every dead second. A 90-second polished video beats a 180-second loose one.
- If the load-bearing flow involves a wallet, blur or hide private addresses and signatures.

## What this template is NOT

- Not a marketing video. Marketing videos pitch; this video shows.
- Not a launch trailer. Hold the pitch for 0:00 to 0:10; spend the rest on the work.
- Not a screencast tutorial. Tutorials are slower than a hackathon viewer's attention.
