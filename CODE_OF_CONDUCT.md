# Code of conduct

Suiperpower adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## TL;DR

- Be respectful. Critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, no slurs, no doxxing, no threats. Online or offline.
- Assume good faith. Disagree without being a dick.
- Keep discussion technical and on topic in issues, PRs, and Discussions.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: this GitHub repo, project Discussions, and any other channel where someone represents Suiperpower (Twitter/X, Telegram, Discord, conferences, etc.).

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to admin@pivy.me with subject `[suiperpower coc]`. Reports are read by Kelvin and handled case by case. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban from project spaces, at the maintainer's discretion. The full enforcement ladder lives in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
